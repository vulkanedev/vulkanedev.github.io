# Prym privacy policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026

Prym is a workout tracker made by Vulkane. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Prym app for Android.

## The short version

Your workouts, routines, exercises and body weight stay on your phone. Prym has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never receive your training or body data.

## What Prym stores

Prym saves what you enter in a database on your phone:

- workouts, with their exercises, sets and notes;
- routines and custom exercises you create;
- body weight entries and their notes;
- with Prym Pro, your weekly plan and planned workouts;
- your settings, such as units, rest timer and theme.

This data never leaves your phone unless you export a backup yourself or search for videos of an exercise (see below). We cannot see it, and nobody else can through Prym.

## Backups

Settings → Backup creates a file with your workouts, routines, custom exercises, exercise rest times, body weights and, if you have made one, your plan and planned workouts, and saves it to a folder you choose on your phone or opens your phone's share sheet. Where the file goes (a folder on the phone, a cloud drive, an email, another app) is your choice, and that service's own privacy policy then applies. Importing reads a backup file you pick and adds its records to Prym on your phone. Export workouts (CSV) and Export body weight (CSV) work the same way, sharing a spreadsheet file of your workouts' sets or your body weights wherever you choose.

## Exercise videos

On an exercise's page, Watch on YouTube opens a YouTube search for videos of that exercise, in the YouTube app if you have it or otherwise in your browser. The search is the exercise's name (for a custom exercise, the name you gave it) followed by "exercise", and it goes to YouTube only when you tap the button. Nothing else is sent. YouTube is run by Google, whose privacy policy then applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Feedback

Settings → Send feedback opens a short form, then your email app with a new message to us containing what you wrote in it, with Prym's version and your phone's Android version filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself, and we only receive what is in it. We use it to answer you and fix problems, and do not share it. Suggesting one of your custom exercises for Prym (Suggest as built-in, on its page) works the same way: the email contains the exercise's name and details, anything you add, and Prym's and Android's versions.

## App updates

Prym checks Expo's update service (EAS Update) for new versions of the app when it opens, and downloads them. These checks send technical information needed to choose the right update: that the phone runs Android, the app's version and current update, and a random identifier the update system creates for this installation. They never include your workouts, body weight or any other data you entered. Expo's privacy policy: https://expo.dev/privacy

## Prym Pro purchases

Prym Pro is an optional subscription bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. Prym uses RevenueCat, a subscription service, to check with Google Play whether you have Prym Pro. Prym contacts RevenueCat only when you open the Prym Pro page, buy or restore a subscription, or, once this phone has had Prym Pro, when the app opens or comes back to the screen. RevenueCat then receives your purchase history for Prym (from Google Play), a random identifier it creates for this installation, and technical details such as the app's version, Android version, language and country, along with your IP address, as with any connection over the internet. It never receives your workouts, body weight or any other data you entered. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Google Play's own handling of your purchase is covered by Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Prym Pro's features, such as the planner, next-session suggestions, the muscle map and trends, work on your phone from what you recorded, like the rest of Prym; nothing about them is sent anywhere. The subscription itself is covered by the [terms of use](https://vulkanedev.github.io/prym/terms/).

## Permissions

- **Notifications** and **vibration:** to tell you when a rest is over, to show the workout you are recording while it is in progress, to remind you to weigh in if you turn that reminder on, and, with Prym Pro, to remind you of planned workouts if you turn those reminders on. These notifications are created on your phone; nothing is sent from a server.
- **Alarms and reminders (exact alarms):** so the rest-over notification arrives on time. Android asks you to allow this in its settings; Prym offers a shortcut there.
- **Run at startup:** so a scheduled rest-over notification or reminder survives the phone restarting.
- **Internet:** to check for and download app updates, and, for Prym Pro, to check your subscription.
- **Billing:** to buy Prym Pro through Google Play.

## Deleting your data

You can delete any workout, routine, custom exercise or body weight entry in the app. Uninstalling Prym, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes everything it stored on your phone. Backup files you exported are not affected; delete them where you saved them.

## Health information

Prym records training and body weight for your own use. It does not provide medical advice.

## Children

Prym is not directed at children under 13.
