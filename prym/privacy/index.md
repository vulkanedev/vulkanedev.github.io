# Prym privacy policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026

Prym is a workout tracker made by Vulkane. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Prym app for Android.

## The short version

Your workouts, routines, exercises and body weight stay on your phone. Prym has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never receive your training or body data.

## What Prym stores

Prym saves what you enter in a database on your phone:

- workouts, with their exercises, sets and notes;
- routines and custom exercises you create;
- body weight entries and their notes;
- your settings, such as units, rest timer and theme.

This data never leaves your phone unless you export a backup yourself (see below). We cannot see it, and nobody else can through Prym.

## Backups

Settings → Backup creates a file with your workouts, routines, custom exercises, exercise rest times and body weights, and opens your phone's share sheet. Where the file goes (a cloud drive, an email, another app) is your choice, and that service's own privacy policy then applies. Importing reads a backup file you pick and adds its records to Prym on your phone.

## Feedback

Settings → Send feedback opens your email app with a new message to us, with Prym's version and your phone's Android version filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself, and we only receive what is in it. We use it to answer you and fix problems, and do not share it.

## App updates

Prym checks Expo's update service (EAS Update) for new versions of the app when it opens, and downloads them. These checks send technical information needed to choose the right update: that the phone runs Android, the app's version and current update, and a random identifier the update system creates for this installation. They never include your workouts, body weight or any other data you entered. Expo's privacy policy: https://expo.dev/privacy

## Permissions

- **Notifications** and **vibration:** to tell you when a rest is over, and to remind you to weigh in if you turn that reminder on. These notifications are created on your phone; nothing is sent from a server.
- **Alarms and reminders (exact alarms):** so the rest-over notification arrives on time. Android asks you to allow this in its settings; Prym offers a shortcut there.
- **Run at startup:** so a scheduled rest-over notification or weigh-in reminder survives the phone restarting.
- **Internet:** only to check for and download app updates.

## Deleting your data

You can delete any workout, routine, custom exercise or body weight entry in the app. Uninstalling Prym, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes everything it stored on your phone. Backup files you exported are not affected; delete them where you saved them.

## Health information

Prym records training and body weight for your own use. It does not provide medical advice.

## Children

Prym is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. If Prym ever starts collecting or sending data in a new way, this policy will say so before it happens.
