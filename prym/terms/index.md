## Health and safety

Prym records your training; it is not medical advice, coaching or a diagnosis. Exercise instructions, suggestions of what to try (such as "Try 82.5 kg × 6") and plans are general guidance, worked out from what you recorded by fixed rules, and cannot know how you feel or whether you have an injury. Train within your abilities, warm up, use a spotter or safety equipment for heavy lifts, stop if anything hurts, and check with a doctor or qualified trainer before starting if you have an injury or health condition.
